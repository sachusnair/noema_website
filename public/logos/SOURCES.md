# Third-party brand marks

These are the official marks of other companies, used to indicate that Noema
connects to their products. They are not ours.

| File | Source | Copyright licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `slack.svg` | [Commons: Slack icon 2019](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Slack_icon_2019.svg) | Public domain |
| `outlook.svg` | [Commons: Microsoft Outlook Icon (2025–present)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Microsoft_Outlook_Icon_(2025%E2%80%93present).svg) | Public domain |
| `gocardless.svg` | The icon `gocardless.com` declares as its own favicon, retrieved 15 Aug 2026 | © GoCardless, no licence granted |
| `monday.png` | The 256px app icon `monday.com` declares as its own `apple-touch-icon`, retrieved 15 Aug 2026 | © monday.com, no licence granted |

The public-domain licence covers copyright on the first two files only. Every
**trademark here remains the property of its owner**, and Slack, Microsoft,
GoCardless and monday.com all publish brand guidelines restricting how their
marks may be used. None of the four are in the simple-icons set, which is why
they are not supplied the way every other mark on this site is.

The GoCardless and monday.com files are the brands' own icons taken from their
own sites, unmodified. No licence was granted for them; they are relied on as
nominative use, the same basis as the rest of this folder. That is a weaker
footing than the two public-domain files above, so if either company objects,
these are the first to pull.

They are used here nominatively, to name a product Noema integrates with. If
either company objects, remove the file and the `file` field on that entry in
`src/content/site.ts`; the tile falls back to a wordmark automatically.

Not held here, and why:

- **Dext** publishes no square mark. Its logo is the word "Dext"; the only icon
  on `dext.com` is a 32px favicon of that same wordmark, set in orange on a
  white square, which would neither read at 16px nor sit on a dark card. Rather
  than run one bare tile, Dext was taken out of the marquee. It is still named
  in the step two panel, where it is a line of illustrative data, not a logo.
